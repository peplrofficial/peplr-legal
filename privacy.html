Privacy Policy

PEPLR · Last updated August 2026

PEPLR is a community app for finding things to do near you and the people who show up to them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we do not do. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. We collect the profile you type in, the things you post, and basic technical data needed to run the service. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, we do not track you across other apps, and we never store your GPS coordinates. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

PEPLR is operated by Mayron Leon Taylor III, Miami, Florida, United States. For any privacy question, contact privacy@peplr.app.

2. What we collect

Location — read this part

PEPLR asks for location permission for exactly one purpose: to work out which city you are in, so your feed is not empty or full of the wrong place.

  • Your coordinates are converted to a city name on your device and then discarded.
  • We never write latitude or longitude to our database.
  • We never track you in the background.
  • You can refuse the permission and pick your city from a list instead. Nothing breaks.

Nothing in PEPLR shows any person's location. Everything on the map or in a listing is a public venue that somebody typed in.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not show advertising, and we do not share data with advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use your content to train machine learning models.
  • We do not run background checks, and we do not verify that members are who they claim to be.

4. Who can see what

Being clear about this matters more than any promise we could make:

  • Your profile — display name, city, neighbourhood, bio, tags and presence record — is visible to any signed-in PEPLR member. It is not visible to the public internet or to anyone without an account.
  • Anything you post — plans, events, buzz, link ups — is visible to signed-in members.
  • Messages are visible only to members of that conversation.
  • Your email address and date of birth are never shown to other members.
  • Blocked accounts cannot see you and you cannot see them.
Messages are private, not secret. Your messages are encrypted while travelling over the internet and encrypted at rest on our database servers. They are not end-to-end encrypted. That means PEPLR's operator can technically read them, and we would have to produce them if legally compelled. We only look at message content when investigating a safety report. If you need genuinely secret communication, use a dedicated encrypted messenger.

5. Where your data lives

PEPLR stores data with Supabase, which hosts on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States. Access is governed by row-level security rules enforced by the database itself, not by the app — so the rules hold even if someone modifies the app.

Our service providers:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage
  • Expo — app builds and over-the-air updates
  • Apple and Google — app distribution and crash reporting

These providers process data on our behalf under their own security commitments. They are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

6. How long we keep it

  • Buzz posts expire automatically — most within a few hours — and are removed.
  • Your account and content are kept until you delete them.
  • Safety reports and block records are kept for up to 2 years after an account is deleted, because deleting an account should not erase evidence of harm to someone else.
  • Backups roll off within 30 days.

7. Deleting your account

Open PEPLR, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, plans, posts and messages. It is immediate and cannot be undone. You do not have to email anyone or wait for us to action it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), Europe and the UK (GDPR), and several US states have these rights by statute. We extend them to everyone regardless of location.

To exercise any of them, email privacy@peplr.app. We respond within 30 days. We will never charge you or degrade your service for asking.

We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

9. Children

PEPLR is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a minor has created an account, we delete it. If you believe a minor is using PEPLR, contact safety@peplr.app and we will act.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form. All traffic uses TLS encryption. Database access is restricted per-row and per-account. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise — but if a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you and the relevant regulator promptly rather than quietly.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will notify you in the app before the change takes effect. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.
