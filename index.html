The problem

Making plans as an adult is exhausting

Six people, one group chat, four days of "what works for you?" — and then nobody goes. Meanwhile there are people ten minutes away doing exactly the thing you wanted to do.

The problem was never a lack of things to do. It's that nothing repeats, so every plan starts from zero.

PEPLR fixes the repeating part. Standing plans happen on the same day every week whether or not you RSVP. No headcount, no guilt, no group chat. Show up when you can. Skip when you can't. It's still there next week.

What's inside

How it's different

No photos. No swiping. No ads.

PEPLR has no profile pictures. Your identity is a coloured mark and a record of what you've turned up to. You're known here for showing up, not for how you look — which changes who joins and how people behave.

🚫 Not a dating app

No matching, no swiping, no DMs from strangers. You can only message people you've joined something with.
📍 Nobody's location is ever shown

Every place in PEPLR is a public venue somebody typed in. We never store your coordinates.
🔒 No ads, no data selling

No tracking, no advertising SDKs, no cookies. Your data isn't the product.
🛡️ Report and block on everything

PEPLR doesn't vet or background-check anyone, and says so plainly. You decide who you meet.

Starting in Miami

One city at a time, on purpose

An app like this is worthless spread thin across fifty cities and genuinely useful when it's dense in one. So it's Miami first — and the first 25 people get in before anyone else.
